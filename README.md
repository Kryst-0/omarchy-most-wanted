# Most Wanted 2005 — Omarchy bar plugin

An animated miniature car on a moving road in the bar. Click for a larger Rockport skyline, car list and controls. Original stylized side profiles drawn in QML Canvas, with different body types, colors, wings and simplified liveries; these are not the game's 3D models or exact vinyl reproductions.

- Wheel over the bar car or the road: previous/next car.
- Left click: open/close the garage; right click: next car; middle click: pause.
- Panel: arrows, search, category filters, random selection, pause and automatic selection every 5 seconds.
- Keyboard in the panel: Left/Right, Space to pause, Escape to close.
- 62 entries: 38 distinct models, 15 Blacklist variants, 8 tuned Black Edition variants and Castrol Syntec Ford GT. Police and traffic NPCs are outside this selectable racing roster.
- English and Italian catalogs; follows the Omarchy theme and reduced motion setting. No background network requests. Automatic selection runs only while the panel is open.

Install: `python3 install.py`. The installer backs up shell.json and any previous installation, then adds the widget to the left section. Disable/remove with `omarchy plugin disable krysto.most-wanted` / `omarchy plugin remove krysto.most-wanted`.

Reference rosters:
